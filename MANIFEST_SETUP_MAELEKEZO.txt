MUHIMU: Buildozer haiongezi kiotomatiki receiver declaration kwenye
AndroidManifest.xml. Njia rahisi zaidi:

1. Fanya build ya kwanza: `buildozer android debug`
   (hii itashindwa kufanya Factory Reset kufanya kazi kikamilifu bado,
   lakini itaunda faili la AndroidManifest.xml kwenye
   .buildozer/android/platform/build-*/dists/<appname>/AndroidManifest.xml)

2. Nakili faili hilo kwenda kwenye root ya project yako kama
   "templates/AndroidManifest.tmpl.xml" (jina hili ni maalum -
   buildozer inatambua faili la jina hili kama override yako mwenyewe)

3. Ndani ya <application> tag, ongeza hii kabla ya </application>:

<receiver
    android:name="org.njiafix.NjiafixDeviceAdminReceiver"
    android:permission="android.permission.BIND_DEVICE_ADMIN">
    <meta-data
        android:name="android.app.device_admin"
        android:resource="@xml/device_admin_receiver" />
    <intent-filter>
        <action android:name="android.app.action.DEVICE_ADMIN_ENABLED" />
    </intent-filter>
</receiver>

4. Fanya `buildozer android debug` tena - itatumia manifest yako
   iliyorekebishwa badala ya default.

Kama hatua hii inaonekana ngumu, mwambie fundi/developer mwenye uzoefu
wa Android/Kivy aisaidie - ni hatua ya "one-time setup" tu, baada ya
hapo build zote zijazo zitatumia manifest hiyo hiyo kiotomatiki.
